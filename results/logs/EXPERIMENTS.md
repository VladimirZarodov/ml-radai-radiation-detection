## Item-7 finding (before any developer data used for detection)
Developer runs are **~204 s long**, each with 1–few sources. Per-source
1-s count profiles are **rise-peak-decay** (median edge/peak ratio 1e-4,
mid-decile CV ≈ 1.27, peak decile spread over 1–8), NOT flat plateaus → these
are **transient strong drive-by-like encounters in very short runs**, not
physically stationary sources. 24/1862 (1.3 %) look plateau-ish; 14/1862 (0.75
%) trigger the `ak_window` clamp (window covers ~whole run). **Consequence:**
developer encounters may be used for **source spectra / templates** (Stage 2)
but are NOT used as detection targets by default, matching testing's drive-by
geometry. Persistent-plateau ablation (item 7 "separate item") = those 24 flat
cases, evaluated only as a Stage-2 ablation if templates underperform.
